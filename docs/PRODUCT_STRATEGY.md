# Product strategy

Status: direction chosen; no product functionality implemented.

Families need confidence about a loved one's recent day without repeated calls. Residents need dignity and control over disclosure. Staff need fewer repetitive calls, not another recording system.

Primary job: “When I am away, help me understand what has been shared about my parent's day and whether I need more information.” Supporting jobs: inspect evidence, understand its age/sharing limits, request a check-in without treating missing data as an emergency.

## Workflow and priorities

1. Identify/select resident with facility context.
2. Read a calm overview with freshness or insufficient-information messaging.
3. Inspect a permitted medication, meal, or activity event.
4. Understand read-only resident sharing choices.
5. Request a routine/soon check-in and receive an honest local acknowledgement.

P0: clean launch, resident selection, permitted overview/event detail, validation, privacy, freshness, independent loading/error/retry, Android accessibility.
P1: structured check-in. Vitals/notes only if permission handling and reliability are complete; displaying every resource is not the goal.

## Trust and omitted scope

Reassurance needs permitted, dated evidence. Missing data is not “all is well.” Never infer diagnosis, fabricate a health score, or leak restricted content through summaries/details. Explain withholding generically, without private content/counts. Family cannot change resident preferences.

The limited sharing-first workflow avoids surveillance. Excluding diagnoses, scores, unsupported trends, and dense monitoring charts avoids a clinical dashboard. Check-in addresses uncertainty without chat infrastructure.

Defer authentication, onboarding, backend, admin tools, chat, push, analytics, AI chatbot/summaries, appointments, complex charts, broad navigation, and elaborate animation.

## Accessibility

Use readable scalable type, adequate contrast, generous targets, semantic labels, plain language, and alternatives to color-only meaning. Verify Android text scaling and screen-reader navigation, loading/error announcements, and focus after retry/resident switching. These checks remain unperformed.
